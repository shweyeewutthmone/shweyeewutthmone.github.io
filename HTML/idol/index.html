About

ATEEZ is an eight-member South Korean K-pop boy group formed by KQ Entertainment. The group consists of leader Kim Hong-joong, Park Seong-hwa, Jeong Yun-ho, Kang Yeo-sang, Choi San, Song Min-gi, Jung Woo-young and Choi Jong-ho. ATEEZ officially debuted on October 24, 2018, with the mini album Treasure EP.1: All to Zero, led by Pirate King and Treasure. Beginning with an ambitious narrative built around adventure and exploration, the group gradually developed into an internationally successful act distinguished by theatrical choreography, intense live performances, concept-driven album series and a particularly strong global fan base known as ATINY.

Formation

The origins of ATEEZ are closely connected with Kim Hong-joong, who contacted KQ Entertainment while still a student by sending the company a letter and examples of music he had created. He became one of the company's earliest trainees and was later joined by the performers who would eventually form the group's permanent eight-member lineup.

International Growth

International touring became an important part of ATEEZ's development almost immediately. The group built audiences throughout North America, Europe and Asia, and its performance-oriented approach proved particularly effective in concerts, where the members' choreography, facial expressions and theatrical stage direction became defining elements of their reputation.Their international fan community helped turn tours and festival appearances into an increasingly significant part of the group's career.
