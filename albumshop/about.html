Where It All Began

It all started with a love for music, memories, and ATEEZ. Inspired by their incredible journey, we wanted to create a little space where ATINY could explore their favorite albums and treasure every era. To us, albums are more than just music; each one holds its own story, emotions, and unforgettable moments. We hope every visit brings you a little happiness and every album becomes a memory worth keeping. ♡

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to create a special space for ATINY to explore and discover ATEEZ albums from every era. We want to make album collecting a more enjoyable experience by bringing together different releases in one place, helping fans find the albums they love and create memories along the way. ♡

Our Mission

Our mission is to bring ATINY closer to the music and memories of ATEEZ by creating a welcoming and enjoyable album-collecting experience. We aim to make every album discovery feel special, celebrating the stories behind each era and the happiness that comes with collecting. ♡

Made for those who treasure every ATEEZ moment.
